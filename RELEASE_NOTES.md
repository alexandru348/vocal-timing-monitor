# 0.7 RC2 Compact

Tested checkpoint: compact monitor, separate voice/instrumental waveforms, audio attack timing relative to the selected Live grid, optional Auto Stop confirmed during recording with the clip retained.

Distributed AMXD unchanged from the owner-tested file; documentation updated after testing on 1 November 2026.
Requires Ableton with Max for Live available. Validated in Live 12 Suite on Windows 11, 44.1 kHz, 100 BPM. Other setups unverified.

Known limits: settings reset on load; noise/speaker bleed can trigger detection; vocal compensation approximate; no musical flow evaluation. Auto Stop stops the entire transport and must be rearmed explicitly.
